The flows of a greenhouse at 3,500 m

What the sensor record — Jan 1 – Sep 5, 2026 — says about heat, water and air moving through the two hydroponic houses at Stok, and where to improve.

01

Heat: it amplifies by day, then empties by night

A typical day’s air temperature hour of day (IST) · hover to read

What to change
  • Cap the top. Automated ridge vents plus passive shade above ≈ 35 °C — harvest the surplus instead of roasting in it.
  • Bank for dawn. 2–4 m³ of insulated water (or a daytime-warmed rock bed) releasing after sunset covers the 2–3 °C gap between the +11 °C night hold and freezing.
  • Choke the leak. Night curtain + sealed envelope. The house holds +11 °C over outdoor but still crosses 0 °C on clear winter nights — that’s the floor to attack.
02

Water: your best thermal asset, undersized and too cold in winter

Monthly mean temperatures gaps = no logging that month

What to change
  • Warm the loop Dec–Feb. A small solution heater, or recirculate through the day-warmed store. The volume is tiny compared with heating air — this is the cheap win. Root zone target 18–24 °C; you sat at 2.9–4.5 °C.
  • Verify the plateau. Nearly 2/3 of consecutive water readings were identical late-season around 23 °C. Credible, but confirm the sensor isn’t pinned.
03

Air: a one-way vapor valve — the house is too dry

Vapor pressure deficit through the day leafy-green ideal band shaded · dashed = 1.8 kPa stress line

What to change
  • Humidify to grow. Low-pressure fog holding 0.8–1.2 kPa from ≈ 09:00–18:00 is the single highest-leverage change in this report.
  • Dry the dawn sliver. In the 05:00–07:00 near-saturated window (VPD < 0.4 kPa, 12–14% of the record), a whisper of vent/heat keeps surfaces dry before fungal pressure starts.
  • Watch EC by day. On extreme-VPD afternoons plants draw harder — check EC/pH morning vs evening.
04

Ground: 1.4 °C of free stability under the floor

Soil temperature by depth (outside the house) winter = Jan–Feb · summer = Jun–Jul

What to change
  • Ride the 1–7 °C band. On any rebuild, sink the house or run a ground loop; even a buried recirculating line at ≈ 1 m cuts both winter cold and summer heat.
  • Deep moisture is a clue. 0.36 m³/m³ at 100–255 cm — if you ever drill for a source, this points down.

If you only do three things

  1. Fog the air to control VPD — expected growth gain outweighs any heating you can afford.
  2. Store the daytime surplus in water — it stops both the 56 °C peaks and the freezing floors with one investment.
  3. Warm the nutrient loop in winter — 18 °C at the roots is cheaper than 18 °C in the air.
Instrument

Log EC/pH of the solution and indoor CO₂ — the two “flows” you currently can’t see.

Verify

Second calibrated temp/RH per house; re-check the 56 °C max and 0% RH readings.

Coverage

East GH has no Jun–Jul record, the hottest season — keep it logging through a full summer.
